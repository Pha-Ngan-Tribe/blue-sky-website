UNDER CONSTRUCTION

Story

Many years ago, an earthquake opened the entrance to an ancient dungeon. Adventurers descended in search of treasure and glory. None returned. Now it is your turn to discover what lies in the depths.

Game Description

Blue Sky is a single-player dungeon crawler with a 26-map campaign. Explore hand-crafted story maps and procedurally generated dungeons, fight monsters, and search for the gear needed to descend farther.

Backtrack through explored rooms, manage melee and ranged weapons, and prepare for the Overlord on the final floor.

Features

  • Procedurally generated maps across most of the campaign
  • Full-featured map editor
  • Fog of war and a map that records explored halls
  • Find keys, equipment, potions, and ammunition
  • Use swords, axes, bows, crossbows, or magic staffs
  • Save and load your campaign, then return to explored rooms
  • Purely handmade pixel art
